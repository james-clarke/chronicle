How it works

  1. 01Sees

    Which window is in front, and when you step away. The app and the title. No keystrokes, no screenshots, no clipboard.

    09:02 Chrome launch brief
09:40 Zoom   sync · mic on
10:12 Chrome LAUNCH-241

  2. 02Recognises

    Every minute, plain rules take a first pass: a ticket key in a tab, a branch in a terminal, a window you filed before. Provisional, and marked so.

    10:12–10:40 → LAUNCH-241
reason: title has the key
0.50 · to confirm

  3. 03Thinks

    When a batch closes, a model inside the app reads a short digest and answers in a fixed shape: blocks, each tied to a task, with a start and end.

    {"ref": 2, "start": 70,
 "end": 133,
 "confidence": 0.9}

  4. 04Learns

    Rename, move or eject a block and the correction is kept with its context. Eject is a hard no. Once a day it tidies the slivers, with one undo.

    "Chrome · Q3 launch brief"
  → Q3 launch brief
eject: not that task, ever
