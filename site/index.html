Connect the tools you already use.

Link your ticket tracker and your project folders. A ticket number in a window title becomes a task with its real name and context attached.

How it works

  1. Capture. A small background process notices which window is in front and when you step away. Any app counts.
  2. Derive. A language model running inside the app groups that activity into tasks and writes a line for each.
  3. Correct. Move a block, rename a task, merge two that are really one. Corrections feed the next pass.
  4. Use. Standup draft each morning, a weekly report, a card per task, a chat that answers from your own history.

Written in Rust, one binary, no browser engine, no server. The model and your history live on your machine.
